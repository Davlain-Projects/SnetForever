# In-game guide views

The quest notice opens when a guide starts or the player accepts a quest in the current step. It shows the live objectives. Select the copper arrow in the top-right corner to fold it into the bearing display.

The bearing display shows the current objective, arrow, and distance in yards. Click the objective title to reopen the notice. Click the arrow or folded map icon to see the destination and your position. The chart draws a trail when the walking navigator or an authored guide line supplies its coordinates. With only a destination, the chart shows the marker. Click the chart's copper arrow to return to Bearing.

The copper handle below each view opens **Details**, with the step list, manual checkboxes, and guide controls. Click the arrow beneath that window to return to Bearing.

The gear opens **Options** in the **Window** section. Choose a **Favorite view** to use it when another guide or quest starts. **Automatic** restores the notice on the next quest or guide. This setting is saved per character. Drag the frame to move the guide. The views share its saved position. **Lock the position** prevents dragging, and resetting positions restores each view’s default anchor.
