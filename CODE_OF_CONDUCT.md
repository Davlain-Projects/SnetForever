# Code of conduct

This policy applies to SnetForever's repository, issues, pull requests, and review conversations.

- Give feedback about the work and explain your reasoning.
- Respect people with different levels of experience and different backgrounds.
- Keep harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and other people's private information out of project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, lock conversations, or limit participation. To report harassment or abuse privately, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
